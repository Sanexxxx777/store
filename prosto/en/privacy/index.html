Privacy Policy

This page explains, in plain terms, what happens to the data you leave in the request form at shulgin.is-a.dev/store/prosto/en/.

Data controller

The data controller is Alexander Shulgin, a self-employed individual.

What data is collected

Only what you enter in the form yourself: your name, a contact for getting in touch (Telegram, WhatsApp, phone, or email), and the text of your message.

Why this data is needed

The only purpose is to reply to your request and discuss the task. No newsletters, no analytics, no reselling of data.

Where the data is stored

Your request arrives as a Telegram message and stays there. No separate database of requests is kept.

Sharing with third parties

Your data is not shared with anyone.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept for as long as needed to answer your request. To have your data deleted, email sanexxx777@gmail.com and I will delete the conversation containing it on request.

Consent
