Data and privacy

Short version: shulgin.is-a.dev/store/prosto/ collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. No form posted to a server, no cookies, no Google Analytics or similar trackers.

The form on the page

The fields work entirely inside your browser: the button turns what you typed into a message and opens Telegram with the draft ready (it is copied to your clipboard as well). Whether to send it is your call. Until you press send inside Telegram, nothing reaches me.

Visit counter

The site counts page views: page path, browser language, referrer and scroll depth. No IP address is recorded, no cookie is set, no visitor id is assigned. It is traffic statistics, not tracking of a person.

If you contacted me yourself

A chat in Telegram, WhatsApp or email is an ordinary conversation between two people: what you wrote stays there, and it is used only to reply and discuss your task. No newsletters, no sharing with third parties, no selling of data.

Who is responsible for this data
