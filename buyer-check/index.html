FAQ

"TrustMRR already verifies revenue."

It does, and we rely on it. We check what the revenue number does not tell you: whether it is falling right now, whether part of it is service, whether the product depends on something that is going away, and what the site and infrastructure look like to a new owner.

"I have a due diligence checklist."

A checklist tells you what to ask. The report tells you what we already found without asking, with proof, and turns the checklist into questions the seller cannot answer vaguely.

"Do you need access from the seller?"

No. Part A is done entirely from outside: public site, DNS, headers, the listing card. Part B is the list of questions where access is needed, with the exact artifact that closes each one.

"Is $199 worth it on a $30k deal?"

It is 0.7 percent of the deal. One of the three sample listings has revenue falling 56 percent against its stated MRR; that is visible on the public card if you know where to look, and it is a $500k difference in price.

"What if you find nothing?"

Then the report says so, with the list of what was checked. A clean report is information too, and it is the one you can forward to a co-investor.

"Who does this?"
